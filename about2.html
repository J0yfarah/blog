I'm a data engineer and researcher working at the intersection of informal economics, NLP, and geospatial systems. Most of my work focuses on making sense of markets that were never designed to be legible to outsiders.

I build pipelines for data that doesn't want to be found.

Currently leading DZ-PROP-TRUTH, a project to standardize and index the Algerian real estate market using automated scraping, Arabic/French NLP normalization, and BigQuery-backed geospatial analytics across all 48 wilayas.

Before that, I spent a few years in logistics data — optimizing last-mile delivery networks across North Africa, which is where I first encountered the "informal economy as data problem" that now shapes most of what I think about.

This blog is where I write about the technical problems I encounter, the tools I build to solve them, and occasionally the broader questions about data infrastructure in economies that are structurally underrepresented in the global tech conversation.

If you're working on something adjacent, I'm happy to talk. Reach me via the links on the right, or just open an issue on any of my public repos.
