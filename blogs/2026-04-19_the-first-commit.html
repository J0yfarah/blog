The First Commit:
Spec-ing Out My Digital Identity

Hello everyone! This is my first time blogging. As I immerse myself in the journey toward becoming an accomplished data professional—while simultaneously navigating the job search—I realized I was lacking a "personal space."

I needed a place to vent, organize my line of thoughts, and share the raw ideas that a resume can't capture.

I built this simple blogging website to create a clearer "mind map" for myself, while also sharing the process with anyone interested in versatile, unfiltered content. Expect everything from rants about the current state of the job market (especially in the Netherlands — the struggle is real!) to documentation of technical projects and new tools I discover.

Zero Corporate Jargon

To put it professionally: I have a busy mind and a wide scope of interests. I believe using this space to write will be as beneficial for me as it is for readers who value authenticity over fluff.

This isn't going to be just "another tech blog" filled with the kind of identical corporate jargon that makes generic blogs a chore to read. This space will have "sprinkles"—personality, freedom of expression, and a bit of honesty.

The Simplicity of the Build

The site itself is intentionally simple: just a list of posts you can read. Nothing too fancy. It was built with love and a few tokens from the Claude free tier (thanks, Anthropic!).

I am a very resourceful person when it comes to finding and building tools—in fact, I’ve already built a tool to help with my journey that I’ll probably blog about later.

What’s Next?

I have tons of things to share with you all. I hope you stay tuned for what’s coming next—from deep dives into the Google Advanced Data Analytics certificate to the technical hurdles of Data Engineering. I hope you enjoy the ride!
