Data Engineer and Technical Consultant with a Master's in Computer Engineering. I build data pipelines, optimize database performance, and bridge the gap between messy real-world data and the systems that need to make sense of it.

I build pipelines for data that doesn't want to be found.

Most recently at Qareeb, I designed and optimized a restaurant analytics platform serving 85+ locations — cutting dashboard response times from several minutes to under one second through TimescaleDB aggregation. At Octadot LLC, I led the data and AI stack for Hurudza, an agricultural platform that won the Huawei Developer Competition both nationally (2024) and at the Northern Africa regional finals (2025).

Alongside engineering, I work as a Technical Business Analyst — translating complex technical requirements into stakeholder-ready roadmaps, managing API integration projects, and producing documentation that actually gets read. I'm comfortable presenting to government panels, engineering teams, and everyone in between.

This blog is where I write about what I'm building and thinking about: data infrastructure in underrepresented markets, NLP for Arabic and Darija, geospatial systems, and the unglamorous engineering work that makes pipelines actually run.
